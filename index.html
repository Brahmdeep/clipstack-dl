A clipboard with a memory

The default Windows clipboard holds one thing at a time. ClipStack holds everything — organized, searchable, and one tap away.

Never lose a copy

Every copied item is saved automatically and survives restarts. Your history is always there when you need it.

Instant search

Full-text search across your entire clipboard history. Find that snippet you copied hours ago in a second.

Pin your favorites

Lock the clips you reach for daily — addresses, emails, templates — to the top so they're always one tap away.

Smart type detection

Links, code, emails, and phone numbers are detected and labeled automatically — so your history reads at a glance.

Quick-paste hotkeys

Ctrl + Shift + 1…9 instantly pastes a pinned clip straight into whatever app you're in. No clicking, no switching.

Free, forever

No subscription, no paywall, no upsell — just one tidy, native ad. Lives in your tray, opens with a global hotkey.
